Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Without signing in, Orlo works fully and keeps everything on your iPhone. If you want your documents in the Orlo cloud, sign in with Apple or Google.

Can I use both Apple and Google?

Yes. In the app you can link both sign-in methods to one account, and merge two accounts if you created them separately — documents from both end up in one place.

Is Orlo free?

Yes. Orlo is free and has no ads.

Where are my scans stored?

Without an account, only on your iPhone. If you’ve signed in, they’re also stored in the Orlo cloud.

How do I delete my account?

In Orlo’s settings. Deleting your account also deletes all your documents from our server.

Can I scan photos I already have?

Yes. Import photos from your gallery — select several at once, and each becomes a page.

How do I fit a file into an upload limit?

When exporting, choose a preset — Portal (up to 1 MB), Email or Archive — and watch the estimated file size, which updates live.

Which languages can Orlo recognize?

Russian and English. Text recognition runs right on your iPhone.

Why does Orlo need the camera and photos?

Only to scan documents and import the photos you choose.

When will Orlo be available?

Orlo is coming soon to the App Store.
