In short

  • No data is collected — not by us and not by third parties.
  • No sign-up and no accounts.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • Everything is stored only on your device.

What Orlo collects

Nothing. Orlo has no accounts, analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs or third-party services. The app does not send your documents, or any information about you or your device, to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

Orlo uses the camera only to scan documents, and photos from your library only to import the pictures you choose for scanning. Images are processed on the device and are never uploaded.

Where your documents are stored

Scans, imported photos and recognized text are stored only on your iPhone, inside Orlo. Orlo has no servers and no cloud sync, so we never see or receive your documents.

Text recognition

Text recognition (Russian and English) runs entirely on your device. Recognized text is not sent anywhere.

Export and sharing

When you export or share a document, it goes only where you choose — for example, to the Files app or to another app. From there, it is handled under the rules of that app or service.

Deleting your data

You can delete any document in the app. Deleting Orlo removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Information from Apple

If you have allowed your iPhone to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports and usage statistics under Apple’s own privacy policy. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Orlo itself contains no analytics.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@hexvale.dev.
