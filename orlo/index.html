Orlo

Free document scanner for iPhone

Take a photo or pick one from your gallery. Orlo finds the page, straightens it and turns it into a PDF of the size you need.

  • No sign-up
  • No cloud
  • No ads
  • No analytics

How it works

  1. Snap or import

    Take a photo of a document or import photos from your gallery — many at once, each becomes a page.

  2. Found and straightened

    Orlo finds the page in the photo and straightens it automatically.

  3. PDF of the right size

    Export a PDF and see its estimated file size live, before you save it.

What Orlo can do

  • The file size you need

    A live size estimate on export, plus presets: Portal (up to 1 MB), Email and Archive.

  • Batch import

    Select many photos in your gallery at once — each one becomes a page of the document.

  • Text recognition

    Recognizes Russian and English text right on your iPhone.

Your documents stay on your phone

Orlo collects no data. Everything is stored only on your iPhone.

  • No sign-up or accounts
  • No cloud — nothing is uploaded
  • No ads and no analytics
  • Camera and photos are used only for scanning
