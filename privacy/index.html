Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy covers the Hexvale website and the messages you send us. Each app has its own privacy policy.

This website

The site uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and doesn’t load any third-party scripts or fonts. If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser and is never sent to us.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don’t receive or use these logs.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to reply and to help with your question, and we don’t sell or pass them on to anyone. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

Our apps

Each app has its own privacy policy that describes exactly what it does with data:

  • Orlo — works without an account; if you sign in, your documents are stored in the Orlo cloud.
  • Beam — no accounts and no personal data; the only request it makes is for the weather.

Your account page at account.hexvale.dev is part of our apps’ service and is described in the Orlo privacy policy: it uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and remember your language.

The fitness app will have its own privacy policy before release.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask for a copy of your correspondence with us, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its processing. Write to us — we’ll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Hexvale is responsible for the data described in this policy. Questions about privacy: hello@hexvale.dev.
