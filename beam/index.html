Projector

Everything the projector needs, in one row

Under your apps sits a row of quick actions — no digging through system menus.

  • FocusSharpen the picture
  • KeystoneSquare up a slanted picture
  • Fit to screenAutomatically fit the picture to the screen
  • PictureBrightness, contrast, sharpness and picture mode
  • Test patternsGrids and test charts for focus, keystone and black level
  • Sleep timerThe projector sleeps by itself after 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes
  • OptimizeCloses apps left in the background and frees memory
  • HDMISwitch to the HDMI input

Focus, keystone, fit and picture open the projector’s own settings screens, so they appear on projectors Beam knows. We test on the Wanbo Vali 1 Pro.

Features

What else Beam can do

Remote buttons

Home opens Beam, and the remote buttons can be reassigned. A small service sees only which button was pressed.

App covers

Each app gets a photo in its mood, with its icon and name — or the app’s own banner, if you prefer. Hold OK on a tile to move or hide it.

+11°

Calendar and weather

An animated sky for the current weather, an hourly forecast, seven days ahead and a month calendar. Weather comes from Open-Meteo.

22:47

Screensaver

Photos, weather or a large clock — thin, analog or flip — or a mix of them. Beam can also be the Google TV screensaver.

Test patterns

Grid and geometry, focus star, black level, gray steps and color bars — to set up the picture on the wall.

  • 15 min
  • 30 min
  • 60 min
  • 90 min

Sleep timer

The projector goes to sleep by itself. A minute before, Beam warns you, and any button cancels.

Privacy

Only your apps on the screen.

  • No Ads
  • No Recommendations
  • No Analytics
  • No Tracking

The remote-button service sees only which button was pressed and doesn’t read the screen. Beam sends nothing anywhere: its only request is for the weather, to Open-Meteo.
